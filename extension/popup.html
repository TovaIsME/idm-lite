Before scanning: use an ad blocker

Scanning opens each link in a background tab and presses the page's own download button for you. File hosts are full of fake download buttons planted by ads, and a page you never look at is a page you cannot check.

IDM-lite skips controls sitting under ad markup, ignores off-site links that are not plainly files, and never reaches into a page's iframes. That is not the same as an ad blocker, and it is not a guarantee.

Run an ad blocker such as uBlock Origin, and check what arrives in your list. If you scan without one, anything that downloads is on you.
